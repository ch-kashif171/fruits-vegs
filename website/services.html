F.A.Q.

Where do you source your fruits and vegetables from?

We work with trusted farms and agricultural hubs around the world, and supply the local market from our shop at the Al Aweer Central Market in Dubai.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes. We offer competitive bulk pricing for supermarkets, grocery stores, hotels, restaurants and distributors. Contact our sales manager for a quote.

Can I place a custom order for specific fruits and vegetables?

Yes. Tell us the products, quantities and delivery dates you need and we'll confirm availability and pricing.

How can I ensure the freshness of your produce?

Every order is sourced, handled and delivered with freshness in mind, and our produce is backed by our freshness guarantee.

How can I contact you for bulk orders or inquiries?
